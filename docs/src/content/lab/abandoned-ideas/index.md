This section is the other half of <a href="{{ '/lab/variants/' | relative_url }}">Tested variants</a>. A tested variant is a change somebody is running on their own machine. Everything here was dropped, replaced or set aside. None of it is part of the build, and you do not need it to build a sorter. It is here so that an old photo, an old forum post or an old message does not send you back to a design the project has left.

Each entry says what the idea was and why it went, as far as the reasons are written down. Where the reason is not on record, the entry says so. The status of each is as of 11 October 2026.
