1. WHAT THIS IS
Custom cable assemblies for one machine. 18 cables per set,
listed in section 3. The drawings are in this package as PDF,
PNG, SVG and HTML (generated with WireViz), each with its own
BOM (TSV) and its WireViz YAML source:
  - power, psu-pigtail, board-power : PSU pigtails PSU1-PSU3,
        load cables PWR1-PWR3. "power" is the overview, the other
        two are the cables it is made of.
  - steppers, channel-stepper, chute-stepper : channel stepper
        crossover cable STP1-STP4, chute cable CHU1. "steppers" is
        the overview.
  - leds, limit-switch : LED feeds LED1a-LED3a, pigtails LED1-LED3,
        limit switch LIM1. "leds" is the overview.
Quote from the per-cable drawings. The overviews show how the
cables fit together.

2. QUANTITY
2 full sets (every cable in section 3 x2): 36 cables.
Quote 2 sets and price breaks at 10 / 50 sets.

4. WIRE AND CONNECTORS
Wire
- UL1007 stranded tinned copper, 300 V, or RoHS equivalent.
- Gauges: 18 AWG (PSU, PWR1), 22 AWG (PWR2, PWR3, LED, LIM1),
  24 AWG (STP, CHU1). 24 AWG is the most the JST PH contact takes.
- Red = +24 V, black = GND on every 2-conductor power cable.
- No UL certification and no IPC-A-620 class required.
  Hobby-grade quality is acceptable; continuity test each
  cable.

Connectors (source to this spec; a part number is the one the
machine was designed against, and any RoHS clone that mates
and crimps the same is fine)
- Board 24 V input: JST VHR-2N housing, JST SVH-21T-P1.1
  contact (PWR1).
- Stepper board end: JST PHR-4 housing, JST SPH-002T-P0.5S
  contact (STP1-STP4, CHU1). Motor end: JST PHR-6 housing, same
  contact (STP1-STP4).
- DC barrel 5.5 x 2.1 mm, centre-positive, 5 A or better:
  male plugs (PWR1-PWR3, LED1-LED3), female inline jacks (LED1a-LED3a),
  female panel-mount jacks (PSU1-PSU3). 2.1 mm pin, NOT 2.5 mm.
  The two do not mate.
- Dupont = generic 2.54 mm (0.1 in) female socket housing and
  female crimp contacts (LED1a-LED3a, LIM1). No keying.
  The moulded arrow on a Dupont housing always marks the
  ground wire: black goes in the cavity next to the arrow,
  and positions count away from the arrow.
  LED1a-LED3a: black at the arrow, red in the other cavity.
  LIM1: the board end is a 3-position housing with only
  positions 1 and 2 populated: black (ground) in position 1
  at the arrow, white (signal) in position 2, position 3
  empty. Fitted, the arrow end goes over the pin the board
  prints GND.
- LIM1 switch end: fully insulated female quick-connect
  receptacle, #187 series (4.75 x 0.5 mm / .187 x .020 in
  tab), 22 AWG. Mates the Omron V-155-1C25 limit switch. No
  soldering. Fully insulated because the switch is SPDT and
  one of its three tabs is left unused.
- Spade/fork terminals (PSU end): 18 AWG, M3.5 stud, 8 mm wide
  MAX so they fit the MeanWell LRS-350-24 terminal block. That
  block is 9 screws, numbered per the MeanWell spec sheet:
  1 AC/L, 2 AC/N, 3 FG, 4-6 DC OUTPUT -V, 7-9 DC OUTPUT +V.
  Each pigtail takes one +V/-V pair (7 with 4, 8 with 5,
  9 with 6). Screws 1-3 are not part of this harness.
- Bare ends: strip 5 mm (0.2 in) and tin, no connector.
  PWR2, PWR3, LED1-LED3 and CHU1 are ordered with one bare end
  because the part they join has its own fixed leads or
  solder pads. They are joined on the machine, not by you.

5. IMPORTANT - CROSSOVER CABLE
Cable STP1-STP4 (steppers drawing) is NOT straight-through. The
motor end is a 6-position housing with only 4 positions
populated:
  board 1 -> motor 1  (blue)
  board 2 -> motor 4  (green)
  board 3 -> motor 3  (red)
  board 4 -> motor 6  (black)
Motor positions 2 and 5 stay empty. The positions are the ones
on the wire diagram in the StepperOnline 17HE15-1504S
datasheet: coil A is motor positions 1 and 4, coil B is 3 and
6. See the channel-stepper drawing.
All other cables are straight-through, wire for wire. The
Dupont leads (LED1a-LED3a, LIM1) are wired by colour as in section 4.

6. TOLERANCES, LABELS, PACKAGING
- Length tolerance +/-10 mm (+/-0.4 in); +/-25 mm (+/-1 in) is
  fine on anything 920 mm (36 in) or longer.
- Label each cable with its ID (PWR1, STP1, ...) at BOTH ends,
  except CHU1, which is labelled once at its board end (its
  other end is spliced to the motor). Every other cable unplugs
  at both ends, and identical cables land within inches of each
  other at the board and at the motors, so they are
  unrecoverable once unplugged. Printed wrap-around laser
  labels or printed heat-shrink are both fine.
- Bag per cable type, ID on the bag. No other packaging
  requirement.
