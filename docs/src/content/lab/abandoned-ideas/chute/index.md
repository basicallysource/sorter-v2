Back to <a href="{{ '/lab/variants/abandoned/' | relative_url }}">Abandoned ideas</a>. The current chute is on <a href="{{ '/hardware/assembly/distribution/chute/' | relative_url }}">Chute</a>. You do not need anything here to build a sorter. These pages are kept so that an old photo or an old message about the earlier chute still makes sense. The status is as of 12 October 2026.

## What it was

The earlier chute had a door flap on the opposite side to today's, and a chute core with 18 heat insert pockets. Builders ran that flap as a variant on their own machines, and it has since become the standard door. The pages below are the build pages for the chute from before that change, copied unchanged apart from the links between them and a note at the top.

## Why it was replaced

The inverted flap was made because pieces dropped out of the wrong layers when the C-channel was not centred over the drop. Its designer found that it seemed to put less strain on the servo (<a href="https://discord.com/channels/1430279849171222682/1437144782819426537/1546967674339463250">the message in the Discord</a>). One builder moved to it on 8 September 2026.

## What changed

<ul class="bulleted-list">
  <li>The core has 21 heat insert pockets instead of 18, on four faces, and the four through holes are gone.</li>
  <li>The 10 inserts of the bearing assembly moved from the race to the holders, and the race now bolts to the core as well.</li>
  <li>The door is a flat flap hinged at the bottom of the opening in the core's side.</li>
  <li>The servo bracket's arms take 3 screws into the core instead of 2.</li>
  <li>The funnel brackets are dovetail slides held by their own spring force.</li>
</ul>
