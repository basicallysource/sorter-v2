**A machine takes two of these bearings and both get the same prep.** Some variants
ship with four rubber feet on one face. They hold the bearing off whatever it is
bolted to, so they come off first. If yours has no feet, there is nothing to do on
this page.

Do this before either bearing goes in: one at the [top interface]({{ '/hardware/assembly/distribution/top-interface/' | relative_url }}), one at the [bottom interface]({{ '/hardware/assembly/distribution/bin-frame/bottom-interface/' | relative_url }}).

## Remove the rubber feet

<ol class="numbered-steps">
  <li>Grip one foot with the pliers, close to where it meets the bearing housing.</li>
  <li>Pull straight up until it releases.</li>
  <li>Work around the housing until none are left.</li>
</ol>

## The finished result

A bearing that sits flat on a bench on both faces, with no feet on either.
