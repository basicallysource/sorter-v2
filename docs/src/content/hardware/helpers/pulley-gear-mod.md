The pulley arrives with a flange at each end. **Only the top one comes off**, and the
lower one stays. Without that, the pulley does not fit inside the Interface spur gear.

Do this before the pulley goes on the NEMA 23 shaft, at the [top interface]({{ '/hardware/assembly/distribution/top-interface/' | relative_url }}).

  <span class="callout-icon" aria-hidden="true">⚠</span>
  <p>The flange lets go suddenly once it is pried far enough. Pry away from yourself and keep your face clear.</p>
</div>

## Remove the top flange

<ol class="numbered-steps">
  <li>Hold the body of the pulley in the needle-nose pliers.</li>
  <li>Get the edge of the bottle opener under the lip of the top flange.</li>
  <li>Pry a little, then move a short way around the circumference and pry again. Work all the way round rather than forcing one side.</li>
  <li>Keep going until the flange pops off. Set it aside; nothing else uses it.</li>
</ol>

The pliers alone will do it if you have no bottle opener: grip the flange and pry it
upwards.

## The finished result

The pulley with one flange left, at the bottom, and a clean open top.
